## Issue cell

**A stray four-byte write corrupts the high half of one heap header field, and every later `free()` from that heap then leaks.** `heap->size` sits at offset 0 of a page-aligned heap; the low 32 bits survive intact and only the high half is wrong, so the write lands at `heap+4`. Masked, both observed heaps are entirely self-consistent — legal `0xd000` size, chunks inside it, ordinary grid. ★ The corrupting word is **not constant** (`0x80000000` and `0x80000001` on two heaps in one run), which reads like a refcount or lock word with a flag in the MSB rather than a marker. Guards: `free() of a corrupt chunk header`, `large-bin lookup returned a non-chunk` / `a wild pointer`, `chunk handed out twice`.
